# Controllers

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, 8BitDo and Steam controllers all work.

## Wired
Plug it in. That's it.

## Bluetooth
1. Click the **Bluetooth** icon near the clock, then **Pair device**.
2. Put the controller in pairing mode:
   - **Xbox**: hold the small pair button on top until the logo flashes fast
   - **PlayStation**: hold **PS** and **Share** (or **Create**) until the light flashes
   - **Switch Pro**: hold the small sync button on top
   - **8BitDo**: check its manual, usually **Start** plus a mode button
3. Pick it from the list.

## In games
Steam translates any controller for any game (Steam Input). If buttons are wrong in one game, open Steam's **Controller** settings for that game.

## Not working?
1. Welcome → **FIX PROBLEMS**.
2. Unplug or turn it off and on again.
3. Restart the computer.
