# FAQ

**Can I install other apps?**
Yes. Open **Discover** from the app menu. It's the app store. Discord, Spotify, browsers, OBS and more are all there.

**Can I break it?**
Not easily. The system part can't be changed by accident, and GO BACK undoes any update.

**Where are my files?**
Open **Dolphin** (the folder icon). Your stuff is in your home folder: Downloads, Pictures and so on.

**Does it run Windows games?**
Most of them, through Proton. See [[Playing games]].

**Does it have viruses or need antivirus?**
No antivirus needed. Just don't run random scripts or programs from the internet.

**Why is it called Sidewinder?**
The Sidewinder is the missile on the F-16's wingtips. Look closely at the logo.

**Who made it?**
A small project built on Fedora, Universal Blue, KDE, CachyOS and more. See the credits in Welcome → ABOUT.
