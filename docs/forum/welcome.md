# Welcome to the Sidewinder forum

Sidewinder is a gaming system for people who just want to play. This is where you ask for help, share what works, and tell us what to fix.

## Where to post

- **Help and Support**: something isn't working and you want a hand. Mark the reply that fixed it as the answer so the next person finds it fast.
- **Game Compatibility Reports**: tell everyone how a game runs. Use the form, it only takes a minute.
- **Showcase**: screenshots, setups, high scores, cool stuff.
- **Feedback and Bugs**: something broke, or you have an idea. Use the bug form so it's easy to fix.
- **Announcements**: news and updates from us. Only we post here.

## Before you post

1. Open **Sidewinder Welcome** from the app menu and try **FIX PROBLEMS**. It fixes most sound, Wi-Fi and controller issues.
2. Something broke right after an update? **GO BACK** in Sidewinder Welcome puts you on the version before. Your games and saves stay.
3. Search the forum. Someone may have had the same problem.

Read the [forum rules](RULES_LINK) too. They're short.

Have fun, and see you in the sky.
