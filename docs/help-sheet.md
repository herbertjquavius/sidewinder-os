# Sidewinder Mayday Sheet

**Start here for almost anything:** open the app menu (bottom-left corner), type **Welcome**, and open **Sidewinder Welcome**.

**Good to know:** updates download by themselves and kick in when you restart. Restarting is always safe, and your games and saves are never touched.

---

### No sound, controller not working, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth dropped
1. Welcome → **FIX PROBLEMS**.
2. Still broken? Restart the laptop.

### A game won't start
1. Close Steam all the way: right-click the Steam icon near the clock → **Exit**. Open Steam again.
2. In Steam, right-click the game → **Properties** → **Compatibility** → tick the box → pick **Proton Experimental**. Try again.
3. Look the game up on **protondb.com** to see what fixed it for other people.

### A game runs slow or choppy
1. Plug in the charger. It runs at full power plugged in and saves battery when unplugged.
2. Welcome → **GAME MODE** on.
3. Close other apps, especially browsers.

### Something broke after an update
1. Welcome → **GO BACK** → **RESTART NOW**.
2. You're back on the version from before the update. Your games and saves stay.

### It won't start up, or the screen stays black
1. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to turn it off. Turn it back on.
2. While it starts, tap **Esc** over and over until a menu shows up.
   (After a failed start, the menu also shows up by itself.)
3. Use the arrow keys to pick the **second line** (the previous version) and press **Enter**.

### The screen froze
1. Wait 30 seconds. Games sometimes catch up.
2. Still frozen? Hold the power button for 10 seconds, then turn it back on.

### Forgot your password
Text your brother.

---

**Don't** type commands you find online into the Terminal unless someone you trust tells you to.

**Still stuck?** Welcome → **WIKI**, or text your brother. Send:
- what you were doing,
- a photo of the screen,
- what you already tried from this sheet.
